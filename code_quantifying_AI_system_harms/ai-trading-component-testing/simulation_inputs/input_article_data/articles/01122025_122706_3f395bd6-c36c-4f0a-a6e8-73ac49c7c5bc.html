Equities Rally Pushes Benchmarks to Fresh Highs as Earnings Beats and Liquidity Fuel Bull Run

A broad-based equities rally lifted major benchmarks to record territory as stronger-than-expected corporate results, easing yields and renewed institutional flows combined to drive multiple expansion. Market breadth widened, cyclicals outperformed and investors increased exposure to growth names after a wave of earnings upgrades and hefty buyback announcements.

Eleanor Grant, Senior Markets Correspondent in New York 01/12/2025 14:30:00

Equities extended a sustained advance on Monday, with the Benchmark Equities Index (BEI) climbing 1.9% to 4,765.24, marking a fresh intraday high and capping a three-week winning streak. The rally was broad-based: the Technology sector led gains, up 2.8%, while Industrials and Financials rose 2.1% and 1.6% respectively. Market participants pointed to a combination of upbeat corporate reports, lower short-term yields and continued inflows into domestic equity products as the key drivers of today’s momentum.

Corporate earnings season provided the spark. Large- and mid-cap names beat consensus on both top- and bottom-line metrics, with several issuing raised guidance. Aurora Technologies, a large-cap cloud and semiconductor services provider, jumped 4.5% after posting quarterly revenue 6% above expectations and raising full-year organic growth guidance to 14%. Crestview Energy rallied 3.8% on a surprise margin expansion tied to efficiency gains, while Beacon Financials announced a $2.3bn accelerated buyback program that underpinned a 5.1% pop in its shares.

Macro conditions have been supportive of equities. Ten-year benchmark yields eased 12 basis points to 3.42%, reducing the discount rate for equity cash flows and enabling modest P/E expansion — the BEI’s forward P/E rose to 17.8x from 17.2x last week, versus a five-year average of 16.2x. Liquidity remained ample: equity mutual funds and ETFs reported combined inflows of $3.1bn on the week, while market-making desks highlighted heightened risk-on positioning and lower hedging costs as catalysts for higher-beta names.

Market internals reinforced the bullish case. Advancing issues outnumbered decliners by a 4:1 margin and 145 stocks in the benchmark hit new 52-week highs, signaling healthy breadth. The SmallCap Growth Index outperformed, gaining 3.4%, as investors rotated into cyclical and domestically exposed companies in anticipation of stronger consumer spending ahead of the year-end. Average daily trading volume rose 12% versus the 30-day average, indicating conviction behind the move.

Analysts said the rally is being underpinned by fundamental improvements rather than a pure technical squeeze. “We’re seeing durable earnings upgrades across technology, industrials and selected financials that justify higher multiple expansion,” said Marco Liu, Head of Equity Strategy at Northern Harbor Capital, who raised his 12-month BEI target to 5,200. Still, strategists cautioned about complacency: valuation dispersion has widened and investors should monitor margin cycles and any reversal in yields that could quickly reprice higher-duration growth stocks.

Key developments

  • Benchmark Equities Index (BEI) hits new intraday high, up 1.9% to 4,765.24 as market breadth strengthens.
  • Corporate earnings beat consensus: Aurora Technologies and Crestview Energy lead notable upside and guidance upgrades.
  • Domestic equity products record $3.1bn of inflows this week; buyback announcements from Beacon Financials add to demand dynamics.

"Earnings quality and improved liquidity have combined to create a constructive backdrop — we expect upside to continue into year-end as long as economic growth remains steady and yields stay contained."

Marco Liu, Head of Equity Strategy, Northern Harbor Capital
