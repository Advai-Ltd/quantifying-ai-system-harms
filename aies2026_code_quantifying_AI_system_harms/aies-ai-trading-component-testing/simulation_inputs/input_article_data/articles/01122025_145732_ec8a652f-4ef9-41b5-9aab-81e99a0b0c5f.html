Agency MBS Rally Intensifies as Spreads Tighten and Investor Demand Surges

A broad-based rally in agency mortgage-backed securities (MBS) gathered pace today as yields fell, option-adjusted spreads narrowed and investors piled into TBAs and agency coupon stacks. Improved housing data, a softer-than-expected Fed tone and easing mortgage rates fueled buying across the curve, reinforcing a bullish case for MBS relative to Treasuries and pushing several coupon classes to multi-week highs.

Eleanor Mason, Senior Fixed-Income Correspondent in New York 01/12/2025 09:30:00

Agency mortgage-backed securities extended a month-long upswing on Monday, driven by a convergence of positive technicals and macro signals that have tilted the risk-reward in favor of bulls. The 30-year Fannie Mae 3.5% coupon led gains, rallying roughly 0.6 point in early trading and pushing yields lower by nearly 9 basis points. Option-adjusted spreads (OAS) on core agency MBS tightened by about 10 bps versus Treasuries, improving relative value for total return-oriented investors who have been rebuilding MBS allocations after a period of net outflows earlier in the year.

Primary and secondary market activity underscored the strength: TBA (to-be-announced) volumes climbed an estimated 18% week-over-week as hedge funds, insurance companies and bank portfolios re-entered the market to capture spread compression and attractive carry. Agency MBS mutual funds and ETFs recorded aggregate inflows of approximately $1.2 billion last week, according to market desk tallies, adding fuel to the rally. Dealers reported constructive tape action across the coupon stack, with 2.0–3.5% coupons especially bid amid expectations of a stabilizing mortgage rate environment.

On the fundamentals side, mortgage-rate relief and cooler prepayment expectations have helped the technical picture. The Freddie Mac 30-year fixed-rate mortgage index eased to a simulated 3.95% from 4.20% a month earlier in market models, while observed CPR (conditional prepayment rate) for seasoned 30-year pools moderated to an estimated 8.5% from 9.3%, reducing uncertainty around extension risk and enhancing convexity profiles for longer coupons. Lower CPRs and a tapering of agency issuance — combined with steady demand from banks seeking high-quality collateral for liquidity buffers — created favorable supply/demand imbalance for MBS holders.

Analysts pointed to the Federal Reserve's more dovish messaging as a catalyst for the move. While the Fed stopped short of signaling rate cuts, comments emphasizing data dependence and a willingness to be patient have trimmed front-end volatility and created a 'risk-on' backdrop for spread products. 'The backdrop is increasingly constructive for agency MBS: a stable Fed stance, cooler prepayments and active re-allocations by institutional investors have created a perfect technical tailwind,' said Jonah Pierce, head of mortgage strategy at Horizon Capital Research. 'We prefer intermediate coupons where convexity gains are most pronounced and downside duration risk is manageable.'

Market participants are also watching the issuance calendar and GSE activity for confirmation that supply will remain digestible. Issuers appear content to pace new large-lot runs, and Ginnie Mae pipeline flows have been steady but not excessive, helping sustain the rally. Strategists at Cedar Street Investments recommended an overweight to 15- and 20-year agency stacks on a tactical basis, citing attractive spread carry vs. duration and reduced prepayment runway compared with the 30-year bucket. Overall, the technical squeeze, healthier relative value and positive macro signals leave the MBS market in an outright bullish posture heading into month-end.

Key developments

  • Agency MBS OAS tightened roughly 10 bps week-over-week as investors increased exposure to coupon stacks.
  • TBA trading volumes rose ~18% week-over-week and agency MBS funds logged an estimated $1.2bn in inflows last week.
  • CPR estimates eased to ~8.5% from 9.3%, reducing extension risk and underpinning gains in longer-dated coupons.

"We view the current setup as a favorable entry point for agency MBS — stable Fed rhetoric, moderating prepayments and renewed institutional demand create a compelling carry-versus-duration trade that looks sustainable into the new quarter."

Jonah Pierce, Head of Mortgage Strategy, Horizon Capital Research
