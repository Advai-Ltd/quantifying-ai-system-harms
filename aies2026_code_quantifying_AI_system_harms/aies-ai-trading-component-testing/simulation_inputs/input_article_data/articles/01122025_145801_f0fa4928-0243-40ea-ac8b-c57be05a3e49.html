Agency MBS Rally Intensifies as Spreads Compress and Demand Surges; Investors Favor Higher-Coupon Pools

A broad-based rally in mortgage-backed securities accelerated this week as Treasury yields eased and institutional demand poured into agency pools. Spreads tightened across the curve, fund inflows climbed, and primary issuance was absorbed quickly — signaling a bullish technical backdrop for MBS with investors rotating into higher-coupon paper to lock in yield while managing convexity.

Eleanor Mitchell and Rajiv Patel in New York 01/12/2025 09:15:00

Agency mortgage-backed securities extended gains on Monday as benchmark Treasury yields fell and investors reallocated into higher-coupon pass-throughs. The 30-year fixed-rate TBA sector saw 30-year FNMA 4.0% coupon MBS tighten roughly 12 basis points in OAS terms on the week, with prices up around 70 ticks from the late-November trough. Market participants pointed to a 15 basis-point decline in the 10-year Treasury to 3.34% as the proximate catalyst, while incoming softer-than-expected inflation prints reinforced the ‘lower-for-longer’ view that has supported duration and MBS carry strategies.

Technical demand was the dominant theme: MBS mutual funds and ETFs recorded estimated inflows of $5.1 billion over the past five trading sessions, according to a compilation of dealer flow data, and TBA volumes surged as mortgage originators pushed supply to the secondary market. Dealers reported that conventional agency coupons in the 3.5%–4.5% range were the most sought-after, compressing bid/offer spreads and boosting secondary market liquidity. The Markit MBS Price Index climbed 1.9% week-over-week, reflecting both price appreciation and spread tightening.

Primary issuance remained well absorbed despite the rally. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac placed roughly $14 billion of conventional coupon stacks last week, with a notable tilt toward 30-year 4.0% and 4.5% pass-throughs. Ginnie Mae pools, particularly those backed by veteran loan programs and FHA originations, attracted outsized demand from bank balance sheets and insurance buyers seeking credit-enhanced duration. Dealers signaled that the buy-side was willing to accept modestly tighter concessions to secure longer-dated, higher-coupon paper — a technical setup supportive of further price performance if Treasury volatility stays subdued.

From a risk-management perspective, market models showed an uptick in projected CPR (conditional prepayment rate) assumptions, but traders argued that the incremental prepayment risk was being priced into higher-coupon pools already trading at premium levels. Credit metrics remain benign: delinquencies and serious delinquency rates for agency collateral held near multi-year lows, easing investor concerns about fundamental deterioration. Strategists at several firms recommended a barbell approach — overweighting higher-coupon 30-year pass-throughs while selectively carrying protection in front-end sneakers to hedge extension risk should rates reprice higher.

Analysts and portfolio managers reiterated a constructive medium-term outlook for agency MBS if the macro environment continues to support lower real yields. Market participants flagged regulatory clarity around guarantee fees and stable GSE cash management as incremental positives for liquidity. While acknowledging the usual caveats around convexity and potential prepayment shocks in the event of a rapid rate move, the consensus view among buy-side strategists interviewed is that the present combination of spread compression, steady issuance absorption, and persistent investor demand points to further upside for select MBS sectors over the coming quarters.

Key developments

  • Agency MBS spreads tightened ~12 bps week-over-week; Markit MBS Price Index up 1.9%.
  • Estimated inflows into MBS funds reached $5.1 billion over five trading sessions, boosting TBA and coupon demand.
  • Primary issuance of approximately $14 billion was largely absorbed, with strong demand for 30-year 3.5%–4.5% coupons and increased interest in Ginnie Mae pools.

""We view agency MBS as entering the early innings of a multi-quarter rally — technicals are favorable, supply is being readily absorbed, and higher-coupon paper offers an attractive risk-adjusted starting yield for duration buyers,""

Marco Alvarez, Senior MBS Strategist, Ardent Capital Markets
